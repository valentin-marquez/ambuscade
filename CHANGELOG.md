# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release, for Minecraft 1.21.1 on Fabric and NeoForge.

- Barbed wire in every wood, and bear trap
- Explosive, gas and teleport mines
- Camouflage net to hide mines and bear traps
- Sentry turret in four tiers, fed with arrows; endless ammo module
- Mortar, aimed with a fire order and fired with redstone
- Keypad lock for vanilla doors and chests
- Perimeter ledger to choose who traps spare
